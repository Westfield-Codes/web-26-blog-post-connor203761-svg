How Ronaldinho Changed My Idea of What Soccer is

Posted on January 1, 2023 by Start Bootstrap
All Posts Soccer

How he changed the way millions of people viewed soccer.

By Connor Slavin | 10/ /26 | Category: Soccer | Comments: [Count]

When Minds Started Changing

I have played soccer for most of my life and I have always loved it. Some people think of soccer as just a sport, but in 1998 a lot of those peoples' minds were changed. The man who did this is named Ronaldinho

How he Expressed Soccer

When people first started watching him play, they realised he played soccer more as an expression of his feelings about it than an actual sport. The whole time he played it looked like he was just having fun, but he dominated the league for many years.

How I Found Him

The first time I ever saw Ronaldinho play was at my soccer summer camp. He was on the Brazil national team in the world cup. I loved how he played because it looked like he wasn’t really trying to win even though he was but more to have fun and be creative. I later found out he also played for many professional league teams and he played the same way during those games.

His Trophy's

  • FIFA World Player of the Year award: Best player in world cup
  • Ballon d’Or: Best player that season
  • FIFA World Cup: Best team out of every country
  • Copa América: Best team in South America
  • UEFA Champions League: League trophy
  • Copa Libertadores: Best team on the continent
  • Confederations Cup: Best of all the world cup teams that won previously and that year

Ronaldinho holding the Ballon d’Or

The End

Ronaldinho retired at the age of 37 because of his age. However, by the time he had retired he had already made such a huge impact on soccer fans around the world including me from the way he played a huge role in my childhood view of soccer. Although he retired millions of people still watch his highlights and show their children.
